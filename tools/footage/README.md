# Footage tools

Scripts from the October 2026 footage bake-off, kept as the reference for the Journey Video Standard's workflow and for the engine work it assumes. They are not part of the build. See [docs/journey-video-standard.md](../../docs/journey-video-standard.md) and [docs/site/footage-study.html](../../docs/site/footage-study.html).

Needs `ffmpeg`, Python 3.9+ with Pillow, and for `steer.py`, `mirror.py`, `wheel.py`, `dirt.py`, `deorb.py`, `vp.py` and `flare.py` a venv with `numpy` and `opencv-python-headless` (pass it to the shell scripts as `PYTHON`). API keys come from `.env.local` (`OPENROUTER_API_KEY`, `HEYGEN_API_KEY`), exported as `OR` and `HG`.

| Script | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `gen_image.py <model> <prompt> <out> [ref.png…]` | One still from an OpenRouter image model, 16:9 by default (`AR=4:3` for anchors and cockpits). `SIZE=2K` for 2400×1792 at 4:3. References over `REFMAX` px (1536; 2400 for an edit of the still itself) are sent as JPEGs of that size; network errors, 429 and 5xx are retried. |
| `submit.py <workdir>` | Submits the shot list to Veo (OpenRouter), Seedance (OpenRouter) and HeyGen (direct); writes `jobs.json`. Edit `SHOTS` for a new scene. |
| `poll.py <workdir>` | Polls the jobs and downloads each clip to `out/` as it completes; writes `status.json`. |
| `steer.py <plate.mp4> <out> <max_deg>` | Steering track from optical-flow camera yaw: `<out>.cmd` for FFmpeg's `sendcmd`, `<out>.csv` to inspect. Yaw is measured near the vanishing point on textured pixels, with passing cars filtered out; a fixed gain gives full lock at `LOCK` (0.25) px per frame. |
| `car-build.sh <car dir>` | Derives a car's `wheel.png`, `glass-mask.png`, `mirror-mask.png`, `side-window-mask.png` and `glass-dirt.png` from its `cockpit.png`, `cabin.png` and `car.json`. The mirror masks come from the boxes in `car.json`, the windshield is the largest keyed region outside them, and the key runs with a 1 px pad (FFmpeg's `chromakey` misreads the border row). See [cars/](../../cars/README.md). |
| `mirror.py <cockpit> <out> [visible]` | Hangs a generated cockpit's rear-view mirror from the top edge of the frame, with `visible` (0.5) of its height in frame and no stalk or console, by cutting it off the flat green glass. Run before the cabin edit. |
| `sheet.py <out.png> <cols> <width> <img>=<label> …` | A labelled contact sheet of stills, for review images. |
| `pace.py <in> <out> [target]` / `pace.py measure <clip…>` | Retimes a plate so the road passes at a steady pace (the forward speed from the optical flow in the road band, normalised for the ground's geometry so a far verge and a near hedge read the same, as the flow a road pixel would have at the band's bottom row, px per frame at 336 wide, smoothed over a second and a half; the target is the pack's cruise pace, 17; until 27 Oct it was the band's plain mean at 3.5, and plates with the same mean ran at speeds 2.5x apart), so the speed carries across every join instead of each scene opening slow. A slow plate gets shorter, a surge is flattened. Frames are picked, never blended: a slowed plate is first motion-interpolated with ffmpeg (`UP`, 3x). Writes `<out>.speed.csv` where the rate clamp (`RATE_MIN` 0.35, `RATE_MAX` 3) left a residual. Pace before ramping; not for holds. `measure` prints each clip's pace at its start, middle and end. |
| `ramp.py <in> <out> stop\|start <seconds> [floor]` | Re-times a plate to slow to a crawl (`stop`) or come up from one (`start`), for cutting a stop with dissolves instead of a generated turn; writes `<out>.speed.csv` for the dials. |
| `handpaste.py <cockpit> <edit> <out-cockpit> <out-hands-mask>` | Takes new hands and forearms (gloves, sleeves) from an image edit of the cockpit still and pastes only them into the still: the original's skin region grown a little, plus whatever differs clearly and touches it (a cuff, a wider sleeve). Writes the feathered mask as `hands-mask.png`, which `car-build.sh` hands to `wheel.py` (`HANDS`) in place of its skin test, since gloves are not skin-coloured. A car with nobody at the wheel ships an all-black `hands-mask.png`, so the matte takes nothing outside the wheel's disc (without it, a strip of the repainted lap that passed the skin test trailed off the rim). |
| `wheel.py <cockpit> <cabin> <hx> <hy> <r> <out>` | The wheel-and-hands matte: where the two stills differ, connected to the wheel. Inside `CLUSTER=x:y:w:h` bright pixels need a clear difference (re-drawn dial bezels would turn with the wheel), and a bite out of the rim (its inner edge jumping outward and back within 40°) is filled in. Warns about missing hand pixels and about any fill. `car-build.sh` runs it. |
| `dirt.py <glass-mask> <out> [seed]` | Dust, smudges and wiper arcs on the windshield, lit in the composite by bright light behind the glass. `car-build.sh` runs it. |
| `vp.py <still> [<out> [WxH [at]]]` | Finds a still's vanishing point from its painted lane lines (white and yellow pixels only, so cracks and shadows don't count), and optionally crops a 16:9 band that puts it at the centre, 45% of the way down (or `at`). For anchors generated at 4:3. `CENTRE=1` for stop stills, whose forecourt lines would mislead it. |
| `drift.py <clip.mp4>…` | Checks that each clip's camera keeps pointing down the road: the vanishing point from the lane lines every half second, flagged at 12% of the width off centre and failed at 20%, with the longest passing stretch for trimming. Exits 2 if any clip fails. |
| `deorb.py <in> <out> <x,y,r>…` | Paints lens-flare orbs out of a still's clear sky with a fitted gradient and matched grain. |
| `car.py <car dir>` | Prints `car.json` as shell variables for `car-build.sh` and `composite.sh`. |
| `flare.py <plate.mp4> <out.mp4> [strength]` | Lens-flare layer on black, for "into the sun" variants only. |
| `composite.sh <plate> <mirrorfeed\|none> <steer.cmd> <out> [flare]` | The three-layer composite: plate, mirrors, cabin, wheel, bloom, then glass and grain. With `none` (the default from `session.sh`) the rear-view mirror is dark auto-dimmed glass, no scene, and the door mirror shows its housing only (its glass is painted over in the car's stills). `CAR` selects the car folder (default `cars/grand-tourer`). Strengths: `BLEED`, `BLOOM`, `REFLECT` (dash in the windshield), `GLARE` (lit glass dirt), `GRAIN`; `CABIN` is the cabin's light level (1 midday, 0.35 night). `VPY` is where the plate's horizon lands in the frame (0.364 of its height). A car with `dials` in `car.json` gets live needles from `DIALS` (a sendcmd track; `session.sh` writes it from the clips' speeds, `CRUISE` km/h, 55). A car with `screens` gets them lit by `headunit.py` (`HEADUNIT=0` leaves them dark). |
| `traffic.py <clip>…` | Finds the vehicles in each plate and the moments they pass us (YOLOX-S from OpenCV's model zoo, run on every other frame and tracked); writes `<clip>.traffic.json`. Needs `docs/sample-videos/models/yolox_s.onnx` (gitignored): `curl -L -o docs/sample-videos/models/yolox_s.onnx https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/raw/main/models/object_detection_yolox/object_detection_yolox_2022nov.onnx` (36 MB; resume with `-C -` if it drops). `session.sh` runs it. |
| `mirrorfeed.py <out> <clip>…` | What the mirrors show: the road already driven, run backwards at our pace (a stop freezes it), with the plates' vehicles painted out, and each vehicle that passed us drawn from behind, going away, from the moment it passed (sprites in `traffic/`, tinted). `session.sh` runs it. `BACK` (60 m) and `HOLDBACK` (150 m, at a stop) set how far back down the road the footage starts. |
| `spray.py <in> <out> <mist.csv> [car]` | Sea spray on the car after the composite: fine droplets on the windshield and a wipe when they build up, beads on the door mirror's housing. A mist level above 1 (`heavy`, 2.5) drenches the glass with more and bigger droplets. `session.sh` runs it for the clips named in `MIST`. |
| `sign.py paste <in> <out> x y w h ground` / `quad` / `fix <in> <out> [x y w h [frame [first]]]` / `place <in> <out> x y w h ground` | Signs and billboards whose text we set (`KIND` distance, guide, park, services, regulatory, shop, gas, lawyer, tourism, warning (a yellow diamond with a curve arrow, `SIGN` left, right or winding); `SIGN` the lines). `paste` draws one on a still (`quad` puts a billboard's artwork into its blank face); `fix` redraws the face in every frame of the clip made from it, following the sign as the clip draws it (or the model's own garbled sign, from a box in any frame), its corners steadied over time (`SMOOTH`, 7 frames: a median throws out a jumped fit, then a moving average), kept square to the frame with `LEVEL=1` (a road sign on posts never tilts, however the posts lean), and painted a little bigger than the model's with `GROW` (1.06 covers its face fully); `place` puts one into a clip that was not remade (holds, a gas stop's moves), carried by the scene round its foot, behind any vehicle `traffic.py` tracked nearer than it. |
| `speed.py <clip> <metres>` | The speed track of a generated straight move at a stop (the creep to the pump), measured from the ground streaming past and scaled to the distance; writes `<clip>.speed.csv` like `ramp.py`, so the dials crawl instead of reading cruise speed. |
| `headunit.py <car> <seconds> <outdir>` | The car's screens as two videos, on black: the radio's face for the console screen (band, LIVE, equalizer lights, station, song, transport, presets) and the station and water temperature for the cluster's centre display. A stand-in for the app's radio; `composite.sh` runs it. The temperature warms to half over `WARMUP` seconds (45). |
| `rain.py <plate.mp4> <out.mp4> [car dir]` | Rain on the windshield and the wipers clearing it, drawn into a plate before compositing. `RAIN` drops a second (900), `WIPE` seconds per wipe (1.6). `RAINCSV` (frame,rain: drops a second per frame) makes it a stretch: the wipers start as the first drops build and stop two sweeps after the rain, and the drops they don't reach dry off. `session.sh` runs it when `RAIN` is set. |
| `mirrorlights.py <mirror.mp4> <out.mp4> [dark]` | Turns a mirror feed's car lights round: red taillights on cars driving away, white headlights on a car following us. `dark` (about 0.35 at night, 1 at dusk or in rain) also darkens the road behind and tints it red. `session.sh` runs it when `MIRRORLIGHTS` is set. |
| `glass.py <composite.mp4> <out.mp4> [car dir]` | After the composite: `WET=1` puts water on the side window (blown back, beading along the A-pillar and top edges) and the door mirror's glass if the car has one; `WETCSV` (frame,wet 0..1) wets it for a stretch and lets it dry after; `NIGHT=1` shows lamps in the side window as small red taillights. `session.sh` runs it in rain, with `WET`, and at night. |
| `still.sh <plate.png> <out.png> [deg]` | One still through the cockpit, to judge an anchor before paying to animate it. Takes the same variables as `composite.sh`. |
| `session.sh <out> <clip…>` | Dissolves clips into one plate, derives steering, and composites. Weather for the whole session or a stretch of it, named by clip (a name or part of one, comma-separated): `RAIN=900` or `RAIN=<clip[:drops],…>` adds windshield rain and wipers; `WET=1` or `WET=<clips>` wets the side glass (rain stretches wet it too); `CABINS=<clip:level,…>` dims the cabin per clip (`cabinlight.py`); `MIST=<clip[:level],…>` adds sea spray, `light` (1) or `heavy` (2.5) (`spray.py`). `FLARE=0.8` adds lens flare when the sun is in frame. The mirrors show no generated view unless `MIRRORS=feed` (then `traffic.py`, `mirrorfeed.py`, and `MIRRORLIGHTS=0.35` at night or `1` at dusk and in rain). A clip named `*hold*` stands still. |
| `vwrite.py` | `Reader` and `Writer`: frames in and out through ffmpeg with accurate rounding, for the passes that redraw a video frame by frame (`rain.py`, `glass.py`, `spray.py`, `cabinlight.py`). OpenCV's decode and its VideoWriter each lost about 1.2 of 255 of brightness, which added up to 7 over three passes (19 Oct 2026); through these the drift is 0.03 a pass. The other frame-by-frame tools still use OpenCV's writer. |
| `cabinlight.py <composite.mp4> <out.mp4> <cabin.csv> [car dir]` | The cabin's light per frame after the composite (frame,level; `BASE` is the level the composite was made at): everything that is not glass scaled by level/BASE, the cluster's backlight kept, the screens dimmed less. `session.sh` runs it for `CABINS`. |
| `seedance.py <dir> <name> <prompt.txt\|text> <first.png> [last.png\|-] [seconds]` | Submits one clip to Seedance 2.0 Fast on OpenRouter (720p, 16:9, no audio) from a first frame and an optional last frame (the same still twice for a hold that loops); appends to `<dir>/jobs.json` for `poll.py <dir>`. `SEED=<n>`. About 0.91 for 10 s. |
| `joins.py <build.mp4> <out dir> [fps [seconds]]` | A contact sheet of every join in a tour (from `<build>.inputs.json`): frames at 4 fps over 3 s centred on each dissolve, labelled with the two clips and the time, one PNG per join and `joins-all.jpg`. A 1 fps strip misses a cross-fade, a freeze or a jump in pace at a join. |
| `qa.py <clip…>` | The automated checks on a tour's clips, as a table: a freeze (frame difference near zero for over 0.5 s while the speed track says moving), a hard cut inside a clip (scene score above 0.3 away from the joins), `drift.py` (not for holds), and on holds the stop height test (people found by YOLOX-S, their heads against the horizon at the frame's centre: pass, borderline within 2%, fail). Writes `<clip>.qa.json`; exits 1 on a fail. |
| `regress.py <build.mp4> [<previous build.mp4>]` | Proof that a rebuilt tour changed only where it was meant to: the input lists beside the builds (`regress.py write <build> <clips…>`, which `build_trip.sh` runs: name, md5, duration) give the changed clips, which must match `EXPECT=<names>`; the windshield of a few frames of every unchanged clip is compared between the builds, shifted by the new start times, and must differ by encode noise only (`NOISE`, 6 of 255). Exits 1 on a regression. |

The cars are in [cars/](../../cars/README.md). The [`journey-footage`](../../.claude/skills/journey-footage/SKILL.md) skill strings these tools together for Claude Code.
