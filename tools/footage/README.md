# Footage tools

Scripts from the October 2026 footage bake-off, kept as the reference for the Journey Video Standard's workflow and for the engine work it assumes. They are not part of the build. See [docs/journey-video-standard.md](../../docs/journey-video-standard.md) and [docs/site/footage-study.html](../../docs/site/footage-study.html).

Needs `ffmpeg`, Python 3.9+ with Pillow, and for `steer.py`, `mirror.py`, `wheel.py`, `dirt.py`, `deorb.py`, `vp.py` and `flare.py` a venv with `numpy` and `opencv-python-headless` (pass it to the shell scripts as `PYTHON`). API keys come from `.env.local` (`OPENROUTER_API_KEY`, `HEYGEN_API_KEY`), exported as `OR` and `HG`.

| Script | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `gen_image.py <model> <prompt> <out> [ref.png…]` | One still from an OpenRouter image model, 16:9 by default (`AR=4:3` for anchors and cockpits). `SIZE=2K` for 2400×1792 at 4:3. References over `REFMAX` px (1536; 2400 for an edit of the still itself) are sent as JPEGs of that size; network errors, 429 and 5xx are retried. |
| `submit.py <workdir>` | Submits the shot list to Veo (OpenRouter), Seedance (OpenRouter) and HeyGen (direct); writes `jobs.json`. Edit `SHOTS` for a new scene. |
| `poll.py <workdir>` | Polls the jobs and downloads each clip to `out/` as it completes; writes `status.json`. |
| `steer.py <plate.mp4> <out> <max_deg>` | Steering track from optical-flow camera yaw: `<out>.cmd` for FFmpeg's `sendcmd`, `<out>.csv` to inspect. Yaw is measured near the vanishing point on textured pixels, with passing cars filtered out; a fixed gain gives full lock at `LOCK` (0.25) px per frame. |
| `car-build.sh <car dir>` | Derives a car's `wheel.png`, `glass-mask.png`, `mirror-mask.png`, `side-window-mask.png` and `glass-dirt.png` from its `cockpit.png`, `cabin.png` and `car.json`. The mirror masks come from the boxes in `car.json`, the windshield is the largest keyed region outside them, and the key runs with a 1 px pad (FFmpeg's `chromakey` misreads the border row). See [cars/](../../cars/README.md). |
| `mirror.py <cockpit> <out> [visible]` | Hangs a generated cockpit's rear-view mirror from the top edge of the frame, with `visible` (0.5) of its height in frame and no stalk or console, by cutting it off the flat green glass. Run before the cabin edit. |
| `sheet.py <out.png> <cols> <width> <img>=<label> …` | A labelled contact sheet of stills, for review images. |
| `ramp.py <in> <out> stop\|start <seconds> [floor]` | Re-times a plate to slow to a crawl (`stop`) or come up from one (`start`), for cutting a stop with dissolves instead of a generated turn; writes `<out>.speed.csv` for the dials. |
| `wheel.py <cockpit> <cabin> <hx> <hy> <r> <out>` | The wheel-and-hands matte: where the two stills differ, connected to the wheel. Inside `CLUSTER=x:y:w:h` bright pixels need a clear difference (re-drawn dial bezels would turn with the wheel), and a bite out of the rim (its inner edge jumping outward and back within 40°) is filled in. Warns about missing hand pixels and about any fill. `car-build.sh` runs it. |
| `dirt.py <glass-mask> <out> [seed]` | Dust, smudges and wiper arcs on the windshield, lit in the composite by bright light behind the glass. `car-build.sh` runs it. |
| `vp.py <still> [<out> [WxH [at]]]` | Finds a still's vanishing point from its painted lane lines (white and yellow pixels only, so cracks and shadows don't count), and optionally crops a 16:9 band that puts it at the centre, 45% of the way down (or `at`). For anchors generated at 4:3. `CENTRE=1` for stop stills, whose forecourt lines would mislead it. |
| `drift.py <clip.mp4>…` | Checks that each clip's camera keeps pointing down the road: the vanishing point from the lane lines every half second, flagged at 12% of the width off centre and failed at 20%, with the longest passing stretch for trimming. Exits 2 if any clip fails. |
| `deorb.py <in> <out> <x,y,r>…` | Paints lens-flare orbs out of a still's clear sky with a fitted gradient and matched grain. |
| `car.py <car dir>` | Prints `car.json` as shell variables for `car-build.sh` and `composite.sh`. |
| `flare.py <plate.mp4> <out.mp4> [strength]` | Lens-flare layer on black, for "into the sun" variants only. |
| `composite.sh <plate> <mirrorfeed> <steer.cmd> <out> [flare]` | The three-layer composite: plate, mirrors, cabin, wheel, bloom, then glass and grain. `CAR` selects the car folder (default `cars/grand-tourer`). Strengths: `BLEED`, `BLOOM`, `REFLECT` (dash in the windshield), `GLARE` (lit glass dirt), `GRAIN`; `CABIN` is the cabin's light level (1 midday, 0.35 night). `VPY` is where the plate's horizon lands in the frame (0.364 of its height). A car with `dials` in `car.json` gets live needles from `DIALS` (a sendcmd track; `session.sh` writes it from the clips' speeds, `CRUISE` km/h, 55). A car with `screens` gets them lit by `headunit.py` (`HEADUNIT=0` leaves them dark). |
| `traffic.py <clip>…` | Finds the vehicles in each plate and the moments they pass us (YOLOX-S from OpenCV's model zoo, run on every other frame and tracked); writes `<clip>.traffic.json`. Needs `docs/sample-videos/models/yolox_s.onnx` (gitignored): `curl -L -o docs/sample-videos/models/yolox_s.onnx https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/raw/main/models/object_detection_yolox/object_detection_yolox_2022nov.onnx` (36 MB; resume with `-C -` if it drops). `session.sh` runs it. |
| `mirrorfeed.py <out> <clip>…` | What the mirrors show: the road already driven, run backwards at our pace (a stop freezes it), with the plates' vehicles painted out, and each vehicle that passed us drawn from behind, going away, from the moment it passed (sprites in `traffic/`, tinted). `session.sh` runs it. `BACK` (60 m) and `HOLDBACK` (150 m, at a stop) set how far back down the road the footage starts. |
| `spray.py <in> <out> <mist.csv> [car]` | Sea spray on the car after the composite: fine droplets on the windshield and a wipe when they build up, beads on the door mirror's silver housing, a speckle on its glass. `session.sh` runs it for the clips named in `MIST`. |
| `sign.py paste <in> <out> x y w h ground` / `quad` / `fix <in> <out> [x y w h [frame [first]]]` / `place <in> <out> x y w h ground` | Signs and billboards whose text we set (`KIND` distance, guide, park, services, regulatory, shop, gas, lawyer, tourism; `SIGN` the lines). `paste` draws one on a still (`quad` puts a billboard's artwork into its blank face); `fix` redraws the face in every frame of the clip made from it, following the sign as the clip draws it (or the model's own garbled sign, from a box in any frame); `place` puts one into a clip that was not remade (holds, a gas stop's moves), carried by the scene round its foot, behind any vehicle `traffic.py` tracked nearer than it. |
| `speed.py <clip> <metres>` | The speed track of a generated straight move at a stop (the creep to the pump), measured from the ground streaming past and scaled to the distance; writes `<clip>.speed.csv` like `ramp.py`, so the dials crawl instead of reading cruise speed. |
| `headunit.py <car> <seconds> <outdir>` | The car's screens as two videos, on black: the radio's face for the console screen (band, LIVE, equalizer lights, station, song, transport, presets) and the station and water temperature for the cluster's centre display. A stand-in for the app's radio; `composite.sh` runs it. The temperature warms to half over `WARMUP` seconds (45). |
| `rain.py <plate.mp4> <out.mp4> [car dir]` | Rain on the windshield and the wipers clearing it, drawn into a plate before compositing. `RAIN` drops a second (900), `WIPE` seconds per wipe (1.6). `session.sh` runs it when `RAIN` is set. |
| `mirrorlights.py <mirror.mp4> <out.mp4> [dark]` | Turns a mirror feed's car lights round: red taillights on cars driving away, white headlights on a car following us. `dark` (about 0.35 at night, 1 at dusk or in rain) also darkens the road behind and tints it red. `session.sh` runs it when `MIRRORLIGHTS` is set. |
| `glass.py <composite.mp4> <out.mp4> [car dir]` | After the composite: `WET=1` puts water on the side window (blown back, beading along the A-pillar and top edges), the door mirror and the rear-view mirror; `NIGHT=1` shows lamps in the side window as small red taillights. `session.sh` runs it in rain, with `WET=1`, and at night. |
| `still.sh <plate.png> <out.png> [deg]` | One still through the cockpit, to judge an anchor before paying to animate it. Takes the same variables as `composite.sh`. |
| `session.sh <out> <clip…>` | Dissolves clips into one plate, derives steering and the mirror feed, and composites. `RAIN=900` adds windshield rain and wipers and wets the side glass (or `WET=1` alone for a wet road); `MIRRORLIGHTS=0.35` (night) or `1` (dusk, rain) turns the mirrors' lights round; `FLARE=0.8` adds lens flare when the sun is in frame; `MIST=<clip names>` adds sea spray (`spray.py`). The mirrors come from `traffic.py` and `mirrorfeed.py`; a clip named `*hold*` stands still. |

The cars are in [cars/](../../cars/README.md). The [`journey-footage`](../../.claude/skills/journey-footage/SKILL.md) skill strings these tools together for Claude Code.
