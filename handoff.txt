## Radio on the photo car: spec for the video renderer

The user chose this on 14 October. Today's radio is the Radio tab of the console panel at the bottom centre of the 3D
dashboard (`src/webview/ui/radioDeck.ts`). When a video pack plays, the dashboard is a photo of a car (`cars/<id>`), and
that bottom-centre spot is the steering wheel. Each car gives the radio a screen instead.

- **Where.** `car.json` `screens.console` is the screen's glass, `[x, y, w, h]` in the car still's pixels (1200×896; the
  view shows rows 0–675, scaled to the panel's width). Grand tourer: `[902, 472, 164, 154]`, about 175×165 px at 1280×720.
  The face sits under the wheel-and-hands layer: the right hand passes in front of the screen's left edge on turns.
- **Two sizes.**
  - The **face** is always on while the car is shown. It has the deck's head only: FM|CB, LIVE, equalizer lights,
    station, song (artist and title on two lines), power, previous, play/pause, next, and presets 1–6. No SET, volume,
    tone controls or lists. Text sizes: station about 15/190 of the screen's width, song 9.5/190, band and LIVE 8/190.
    `tools/footage/headunit.py` is the stand-in (see fx-19).
  - The **full radio** is the whole deck, in a panel that slides up from the bottom over the lower dashboard, right of
    centre, with the road still in view (fx-18 mockup C). A click on the face (not on a transport or preset button) or
    R opens it; R, Esc or a click outside closes it. It can keep the console's tabs, which makes the screen the
    console's home in the photo car.
- **Lights.** The equalizer lights run whenever the face is on screen, not only on the Radio tab (`Cockpit.syncRadio`
  today). Reduced motion still stills them.
- **CB on the face.** The co-pilot's name and status, and a dot per crew agent, amber while one waits or needs help.
- **Night.** Screens dim to 0.55 + 0.45 × the cabin light (about 70% at night).
- **Cluster display.** `screens.cluster` (grand tourer `[562, 458, 78, 68]`) shows the station and band, and the water
  temperature as a bar from C to H that warms to half over the first 45 s. The temperature is the car's, not the radio's.
