Dashboard

The dashboard

Dash mode, while you code and an agent works, with a crew agent in the side mirror. Press D from anywhere to come here.

ControlFed byClick or key
  1. 1Display modeYour choice. Each screen remembers its own.Tour: the view, with a strip of the trip. Dash: this dashboard. Work: your work panels; in VS Code it takes you back to the editor. Keys T D W.
  2. 2DestinationsThe journey packs.Opens the destination board, to pick or change a journey. Key G.
  3. 3PassportYour trips, stamps and travel memories.Opens your Passport. Key P.
  4. 4Capture my workplaceThe view and the dashboard as they are now.Saves a picture, with private details left out. Key C.
  5. 5SoundYour sound setting.Turns the tour's sound on or off: engine, road, weather and the Focus Mix. The radio has its own power and volume. Key M.
  6. 6SettingsThis screen’s settings.Opens Settings: display, alerts, sound, accessibility and performance. Key S; ? lists every key.
  7. 7Rear-view mirrorNothing. It is a plain mirror, kept clear of text.Only its bell does anything.
  8. 8Alerts bellThe loudest alert: red when something stopped the car, orange when something needs you, blue for files not yet committed. Dark when there is nothing.Opens the dashboard on the Alerts tab.
  9. 9Side mirrorsCrew agents: each sub-agent your agent starts, until it reports back.Hover one for what it is doing.
  10. 10Windshield lineNews only: the place at a stop, a tunnel, a town on the route, departing and arriving. Clear while you cruise.Nothing to press.
  11. 11ActivityYour coding: edits, saves, moving around and focus, fading over about 15 s. Red zone from 75%.Nothing to press.
  12. 12Agents & ToolsYour agents and tools: agent events, commands starting and running, agents at work. Red zone from 75%.Nothing to press.
  13. 13Screen tabsNav, Radio, PRs, Trip, Console, and the bell for Alerts. The PRs count turns amber when one needs you.Switches the screen. Each tab has its own section below. R opens Radio.
  14. 14JourneyHow much of the journey is left, with the percent done. Full again after you refuel.Nothing to press.
  15. 15ContextHow much of your agent's context window is left, as Claude Code reports it. Red under 25%.Nothing to press.
  16. 16ChangesFiles changed and not committed, from Git, like the Source Control badge. ↑N: commits to push. Red past 20 files.Opens Source Control in VS Code.
  17. 17IssuesErrors and warnings in Problems, and failing checks. Red past three issues.Nothing to press.
  18. 18Warning lightsCheck engine (a failing test, build or lint), warnings, hazard (pulled over), agent waiting, your turn (if you chose the blue light), headlights (dusk, night, rain, fog).Nothing to press.
  19. 19GearP stopped, R reviewing a diff, N thinking, D coding, an agent at work or verifying. The hint beside it says which.Nothing to press.
  20. 20GloveboxYour project's docs: README, CONTRIBUTING, CHANGELOG and docs/.Lists the docs; picking one opens it in VS Code, Markdown in its preview.
Dashboard

Stopped for you

Your agent asked a question, so the car pulled into a scenic stop. It drives on when you answer.

ControlFed byClick or key
  1. 1Alerts bellThe loudest alert: red when something stopped the car, orange when something needs you, blue for files not yet committed. Dark when there is nothing.Opens the dashboard on the Alerts tab.
  2. 2Alert pillThe alert that stopped the car, coloured like the bells.Takes you where you act: the agent's terminal (else the editor), the PR on GitHub, or Problems.
  3. 3Stop cardWhy the car stopped: a question, a plan or approval, an agent that stopped, a failing build, your turn, a breather, refuelling, or parked.When parked, Resume journey. Otherwise reply to your agent or get back to work, and the drive resumes.
  4. 4Windshield lineThe stop’s place. The card above says why.Nothing to press.
  5. 5Agent waiting lightAn agent waiting for you.Nothing to press.
  6. 6GearP while stopped.Nothing to press.

Console tab

Radio, FM, playing

Internet radio. A real station plays through VibeTour's local server (the CLI companion here; in VS Code, the extension). Searches and streams go out from there, never from the tour page. A Companion Display keeps playing; in VS Code the radio stops when the panel closes. R opens it.

ControlFed byClick or key
  1. 1Band: FM and CBYour last choice.FM plays internet radio. CB lists your agents; an amber dot on it means one is waiting.
  2. 2Equalizer lightsThe music playing, this radio or the tour’s Focus Mix: columns rise with the level and the beat.Nothing to press. Off in Settings › Sound.
  3. 3LIVEThe stream: OFF, tuning, LIVE (pulsing with the beat), or NO SIGNAL.Nothing to press.
  4. 4StationThe station you tuned.Nothing to press.
  5. 5SongThe song title the station sends inside its stream.Nothing to press.
  6. 6StreamCodec, bitrate and the station’s country.Nothing to press.
  7. 7PowerOn or off.Turns the radio on or off. It plays even with the tour’s sound off.
  8. 8Previous presetYour presets.Tunes the previous saved preset.
  9. 9Play / stopPlaying or stopped.Starts or stops the station.
  10. 10Next presetYour presets.Tunes the next saved preset.
  11. 11VolumeThe radio’s own volume, apart from the tour’s.Drag. Saved when you let go.
  12. 12Presets 1–6Six saved stations; SomaFM channels to start.Tap to tune. Hold, or Shift-click, to save the station playing.
  13. 13SETArmed or not.Press, then press a preset to save the station playing there.
  14. 14ToneBass, treble, fader (rear to front) and balance (left to right).Drag each one.
  15. 15SurroundOn or off.Widens the sound around you.
  16. 16ST MIXStereo, wide or mono.Cycles through the three.

Console tab

Alerts

Everything that needs you on the left; the file you are in and the files not yet committed on the right. Each side scrolls on its own. The mirror’s bell opens this tab.

ControlFed byClick or key
  1. 1Alerts tabLit like the mirror’s bell.Opens this tab.
  2. 2Needs youOne pill per alert while it holds, most urgent first: questions, plans, approvals, stops, your turn, PRs. Each kind has a switch in Settings › Alerts.Takes you where you act: the agent's terminal (else the editor), the PR on GitHub, or Problems.
  3. 3EditingThe file open in your editor.Opens it in VS Code.
  4. 4ChangedFiles changed and not committed, from Git. Names are hidden in Streaming Mode.Opens the file in VS Code.
